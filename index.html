Our mission

Helping local communities make sustainability real.

The Sustainable Development Lab is a student-led research group that supports local businesses and organizations in implementing sustainable initiatives through experimental research and evidence-based recommendations.

Our vision

Building a community that grows sustainably.

A community where local organizations can have access to the best ideas in sustainability, see evidence and keep doing what works. We hope to build a community of students and researchers that truly care about community partnership and sustainability.

We run the experiments

We compare different solutions directly, to help identify what really works, working directly with partners in developing solutions.
