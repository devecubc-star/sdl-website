Exploring a circular economy model for sports equipment in British Columbia

We ask a simple question, what are the most common barriers to youth sports in Canada, and B.C.? We believe that a big challenge is getting sports equipment. While a surplus of gear sits unused, many communities might be in great need of them.

We're exploring whether a connected system could cut down on this waste and open sports up to more kids in B.C.
